About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Educational and School Psychology at Beijing Normal University (expected July 2027), working with Prof. Xinchun Wu at the Research Center of Children's Reading and Learning. During 2025–2026, I was a China Scholarship Council–funded joint doctoral student at the University of Minnesota, working with Prof. Panayiota Kendeou.

My research asks how children learn to read — and read to learn — across print and digital environments.

Research Interests

  • Cognitive and emotional foundations of reading development — how skills like theory of mind shape children's reading comprehension.
  • Multiple-text comprehension and source evaluation — how children and adolescents judge, select, and integrate information from competing sources.
  • Reading and information literacy in the digital age — how adolescents search, evaluate, and make sense of information on the open web.
